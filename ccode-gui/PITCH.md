## The Problem (30 seconds)

Right now, across Australia, there are hundreds of companies with a problem most people would envy: **more demand than they can serve.**

They've built something people genuinely want — a product, a service, a brand with a waitlist. But their systems can't keep up. The founder is the bottleneck. The team is stretched. They're turning down work, burning out their best people, or saying "not yet" to customers who are ready now.

And every agency in Australia is pitching them AI transformation.

## The Insight (15 seconds)

These companies don't need new ideas. They don't need a digital transformation roadmap. They don't need AI bolted onto a broken process.

**They need their growth liberated.** The growth is already there — trapped behind a system, a process, or a bandwidth constraint that working harder won't fix.

## What Circles Does (30 seconds)

Circles runs a **90-day Growth Residency**.

We identify the bottleneck. We build the operational system that removes it. Your team learns it. We measure the outcome against a baseline we agreed on day one. Then we leave.

**You keep the system. Your team runs it. We prove the return.**
