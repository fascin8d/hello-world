## 1. The Anatomy of AI Fatigue — Quantified

### The Trough is official
- **Gartner Hype Cycle 2025:** GenAI has entered the **Trough of Disillusionment**. The average company invested $1.9M in GenAI projects in 2024, but **less than 30% of CEOs were happy with their returns**. ([Gartner via Procurement Magazine](https://procurementmag.com/news/gartner-generative-ai-trough-disillusionment))
- **~30% of generative AI projects are expected to be discontinued** after pilot stages, mainly due to data quality issues and unclear business value. ([Forrester/DigitalCommerce360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/01/22/forrester-b2b-buying-ai-2026/))
- **McKinsey State of AI 2025:** Only 39% report any EBIT impact from AI. Only ~1/3 report scaling AI across the organisation. Usage is up but value at scale remains elusive. ([McKinsey](https://humansareobsolete.com/articles/mckinsey-state-ai-2025-enterprise-agent-scaling-challenges-november))

### B2B buyers are sceptical
- **28% of procurement respondents** felt *less* confident in a decision because of inaccurate AI output (vs 19% of all buyers). ([6sense B2B Buyer Experience Report 2025](https://6sense.com/science-of-b2b/buyer-experience-report-2025/))
- **22% said they wasted time** because of poor AI information (vs 17% overall).
- While 73% of B2B buyers now use AI tools in purchase research, only **36% felt more confident** they made a better-informed decision. **20% said they were less confident** due to unreliable info. ([6sense](https://6sense.com/science-of-b2b/buyer-experience-report-2025/))

### Consumers are revolting
- **Consumer enthusiasm for AI-generated creator content dropped from 60% (2023) to 26% (2025).** ([eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/exclusive--ai-slop-threat-creator-economy))
- **52% of consumers reduce engagement** with content they believe is AI-generated — affecting CTR, time-on-page, and conversion. ([SmythOS/AI Content Trust Gap](https://smythos.com/thought-leadership/the-ai-content-trust-gap-why-73-of-consumers-can-spot-and-reject-ai-generated-marketing/))
- **32% of US/UK consumers say AI is negatively disrupting the creator economy**, up from 18% in 2023. ([Billion Dollar Boy via eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/consumers-feel-increasingly-negative-about-ai-creator-economy))
- **59.9% of consumers now doubt the authenticity of online content.** ([AutoFaceless](https://autofaceless.ai/blog/ai-content-creation-statistics-2026))
- A 2025 study from the **Nuremberg Institute for Market Decisions** found that simply labeling an ad as AI-generated makes people see it as **less natural, less useful**, and **lowers willingness to purchase**. ([NIM](https://www.nim.org/en/publications/detail/transparency-without-trust))

### The perception gap is enormous
- **73% of marketers believe AI content performs better**, but only **26% of consumers** now prefer AI-generated creator content. ([eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/consumers-rejecting-ai-generated-creator-content))
- **77% of marketers** believe AI effectively crafts emotionally resonant content. Only **33% of consumers** agree. ([eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/consumers-rejecting-ai-generated-creator-content))
- **98% of consumers** agree that "authentic" images and videos are pivotal in establishing trust. ([Getty Images](https://newsroom.gettyimages.com/en/getty-images/nearly-90-of-consumers-want-transparency-on-ai-images-finds-getty-images-report))

**Summary:** AI fatigue is not a vibe — it's a quantified, accelerating phenomenon. The trough is here. Buyers are sceptical. Consumers actively punish AI-generated content. The gap between what marketers believe and what buyers feel is a canyon.

## 2. Messaging Patterns That Are Losing

### Pattern 1: "AI-Powered X"
The default agency headline. **AI washing** — deliberately or negligently exaggerating AI capabilities — is now a credibility destroyer. In 2026, marketing leaders are asking whether it's "real ML or just whitelabeled ChatGPT prompts." ([Catersource/B2B Marketing Trends 2026](https://www.catersource.com/tools-technology/the-top-three-ai-trends-in-b2b-marketing-for-2026))

### Pattern 2: "We leverage AI to transform your business"
Generic, unfalsifiable, and identical to 10,000 other agencies. **94% of marketers agree trust is critical** to B2B success, but vapid claims erode it. ([KLIQ Interactive](https://kliqinteractive.com/insights/b2b-reports-benchmarks-and-statistics-2025-2026/))

### Pattern 3: "Our proprietary AI model..."
Most don't have one. Buyers know this. The term "proprietary AI" has become a red flag for bullshit, especially after the SEC began investigating AI washing claims. ([SAGE Journals — AI Booing cycle](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/14707853251379285))

### Pattern 4: Leading with automation over outcomes
"We automate your workflows" leads to the fear response ("you want to replace my people") rather than the desire response ("you'll make my people dangerous"). AI-enhanced companies grew at only 16% — *below* non-AI companies at 19%. ([Maxio B2B Growth Report 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260129012461/en/B2B-SaaS-and-AI-Growth-Remains-Strong-but-Has-Become-More-Selective-According-to-New-Maxio-Industry-Report))

### Pattern 5: "Seamless AI integration"
Consumers experience disgust when they believe emotional content has been generated by AI and it affects their brand relationship. "Seamless" is the opposite of transparent. ([NIM](https://www.nim.org/en/publications/detail/transparency-without-trust))

## 3. Messaging Patterns That Are Winning

### Company 1: 37signals / Basecamp
**Positioning:** "The saner, calmer, organized way to manage your projects."
**Tactic:** 25 years of opinionated writing (books, blog, HEY World). Zero AI claims. Lead with *philosophy* — calm over chaos, small over big, profit over growth-at-all-costs. Profitable for 25 straight years, zero debt. Recently saved $1M by *leaving* the cloud. ([37signals](https://37signals.com/00))
**Why it works:** They sell a worldview, not a feature set. Founders who share that worldview self-select in.

### Company 2: iHeartMedia — "Guaranteed Human"
**Positioning:** Explicitly anti-AI in creative. Their research found **90% of listeners want media created by humans**, even listeners who use AI tools themselves. Launched "guaranteed human" campaign late 2025. ([CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2025/12/16/business/anti-ai-backlash-nightcap))
**Why it works:** Betting on the authenticity premium at exactly the moment the market is saturated with AI slop.

### Company 3: Aerie (American Eagle)
**Positioning:** Recommitted to never use AI to generate or alter bodies in their images. Part of a 2026 wave of brands including Equinox and Almond Breeze calling out "AI slop and tech gimmicks" in ad campaigns. ([AdWeek](https://www.adweek.com/creativity/in-2026-brands-are-virtue-signaling-about-ai/))
**Why it works:** Specificity. Not "we're authentic" — "we will never digitally alter a body."

### Company 4: The Tyee (independent journalism)
**Positioning:** Published a no-AI policy: "We will not publish journalism that is written or generated by AI."
**Why it works:** A declaration, not a slogan. Concrete, falsifiable, and opinionated.

### Company 5: Non-AI B2B SaaS outperformers
**Maxio B2B Growth Report (Jan 2026):** Non-AI companies grew at 19%, AI-enhanced lagged at 16%. Truly AI-led (AI core to product) grew fastest at 21%, but "AI as a bolt-on" was the *worst* strategy. The lesson: if AI isn't genuinely core to your product, don't pretend it is. ([Maxio/BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260129012461/en/B2B-SaaS-and-AI-Growth-Remains-Strong-but-Has-Become-More-Selective-According-to-New-Maxio-Industry-Report))
**Why it works:** Let the outcome speak. If tech is the enabler, keep it in the engine room.

## 4. Vocabulary Shift Table

| Words AI-fatigued buyers REJECT | Words they RESPOND to |
|---|---|
| AI-powered | Handcrafted / Purpose-built |
| Leverage AI | Make your team dangerous |
| Seamless | Transparent / Honest |
| Transform | Build / Strengthen / Free up |
| Cutting-edge | Proven / Battle-tested |
| Next-generation | Right-sized / Fit-for-purpose |
| Disrupt | Serve / Amplify |
| Solution | The specific thing it does (name it) |
| Unlock potential | "Your team ships by Friday" (concrete) |
| Synergy | Collaboration / Partnership |
| Revolutionize | Improve / Fix / Finish |
| End-to-end | "From signup to first value in 3 days" (specific) |
| Journey | "90 days. Measured. Done." |
| Empower | Equip / Train / Back |
| Innovation | Craft / Rigour / Discipline |
| Scalable | "Works at 10 clients. Works at 100." |

**The pattern:** Replace abstraction with specificity. Replace grandiosity with modesty. Replace tech-forward with outcome-forward. Replace passive ("unlocking") with active ("your team ships").

## 5. Three Positioning Statements Circles Could Use Tomorrow

### Option A — The liberation framing
> **"We help companies that are too good to stay small."**
> You've built something people want. You just can't deliver it fast enough. We build the systems that let your team serve every customer who's waiting — without losing what made you different in the first place.

### Option B — The anti-agency framing
> **"No AI pitch decks. No transformation roadmaps. Just your team, amplified."**
> You already know what works. We make it work at scale. 90 days. Measured outcomes. Your people, not ours, run it when we leave.

### Option C — The craft framing
> **"Your demand outgrew your systems. We fix that."**
> You're turning down work, burning out your best people, or saying 'not yet' to customers who are ready now. We build the operational backbone — not a new strategy, not a new brand — so your quality travels at the speed of your demand.
