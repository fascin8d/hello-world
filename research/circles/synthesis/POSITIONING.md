## The One-Paragraph Position

Circles is a growth residency for companies that do things differently. We work with founder-led businesses that already have more demand than they can serve — the ones with waitlists, lead-time complaints, and teams stretched thin. In a 90-day engagement, we build the operational backbone that lets their quality travel at the speed of their demand. We don't sell AI. We don't sell transformation. We build systems their team runs independently — and we prove the return within 9 months. Our clients are the companies too good to stay small. We make sure they don't have to.

## The Strategic Narrative (Andy Raskin format)

### 1. The shift
The companies winning in 2026 aren't the ones with the most tools or the biggest teams. They're the ones who figured out how to amplify what was already different about them — while everyone else was busy chasing the next platform, the next AI feature, the next "transformation."

### 2. The old game (the enemy)
**The Growth-Industrial Complex.** Buy more tools. Hire more people. Follow the playbook. Slap "AI-powered" on it. Hope something sticks.

The result: bloated tech stacks nobody uses (McKinsey: only 39% of companies report EBIT impact from AI). Burnt-out founders (54% reported burnout in the last 12 months). Teams drowning in platforms. Growth that comes at the expense of the thing that made the company special.

### 3. The new game
**Liberate the growth already trapped inside the business.**

These companies already have demand. They already have a team. They already have something people want. What they don't have is the operational backbone that lets their quality travel at the speed of their demand.

### 4. The promised land
Your team serves every customer who's waiting. Your founder gets weekends back. Your quality doesn't dilute as you grow. And you can prove — with numbers, measured against a baseline you agreed on day 1 — that the investment returned itself.

### 5. How Circles gets you there
A 90-day growth residency. We identify the bottleneck. We build the system. Your team runs it. We measure the outcome. Then we leave.

## Who We Serve (from R3)

### The four archetypes

1. **The Founder-Led Craft Scale-Up** — Design-forward DTC, premium brands, healthtech. Revenue $3M–$30M. The founder IS the brand and IS the bottleneck.
2. **The Differentiated Professional Services Firm** — Boutique consulting, specialist law/accounting, niche engineering. Revenue $5M–$50M. Reputation-driven. Every referral expects the partner's personal attention.
3. **The Operator-Led Specialist Business** — Premium trades, allied health groups, specialist manufacturing, hospitality groups. Revenue $2M–$20M. Hands-on. Quality is personal.
4. **The Vertical SaaS / Productised Service** — Niche software with cult following. $2M–$30M ARR. NPS is insane, churn is tiny, and they can't onboard fast enough.

### The trigger moment
"The moment a founder realises they are the ceiling — when demand exists but the business can't serve it because of a system, process, or bandwidth constraint that the founder can no longer solve by working harder."

### Who we refuse
- Pre-revenue companies (no demand to amplify)
- Commodity competitors (nothing different to amplify)
- Enterprise bureaucracies (can't move fast enough for 90-day ROI)
- Lifestyle businesses by choice (no growth intent)

## The Commercial Model (from R4)

### The Hybrid (recommended)
- **$150k AUD base fee** — covers Circles' delivery costs. Paid in 3 monthly instalments.
- **$100k AUD success fee** — paid when agreed metric is hit (e.g., "$250k incremental revenue within 9 months from engagement start").
- **Total: $250k AUD if successful. $150k AUD if not.**

### The qualification test (5 gates)
1. Run-rate revenue ≥ $3M AUD annualised
2. Observable signal of unmet demand (waitlist, lead times, turn-aways)
3. One controllable lever Circles can own end-to-end
4. Founder + ops lead available for weekly 30-min check-in for 90 days
5. Client signs baseline agreement (metric, method, attribution) on day 1

### R&D Tax Incentive kicker
For eligible companies (<$20M turnover), 30–70% of the engagement may qualify for the 43.5% RDTI rebate, reducing the effective net cost to **$165k–$217k AUD**. (Source: business.gov.au, Bulletpoint, Pattens)

## What We Still Don't Know (Open Questions)

1. **Primary buyer interviews needed.** All research is secondary (surveys, reports, search data). Need 10–15 conversations with founders matching the ICP archetypes to validate the trigger moment, the vocabulary, and the price sensitivity.
2. **Circles' own case studies.** The strongest proof is a real engagement. Cohort 1 is the make-or-break.
3. **The exact R&D Tax Incentive eligibility** for a "typical Circles engagement." Need a tax advisor to model this.
4. **Competitive landscape.** Who else in AU is positioning similarly? Josephmark and Humaan are adjacent but not identical. Need a competitive teardown.
5. **The founder's personal story.** The best B2B positioning is founder-led (77% of customers more likely to buy if CEO is active on social). The narrative needs the founder's voice, not a consultant's.

1. **Post the H1 on LinkedIn** as a question: "What would you do if your company was too good to stay small — but your systems couldn't keep up with demand?" See who responds. That's your ICP.
